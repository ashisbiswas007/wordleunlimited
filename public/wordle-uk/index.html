How to play Wordle

Find the hidden UK English word within 6 tries, type a word of 5 letters or choose the difficulty by changing the word length from the settings. From 3 letters to 7 letters words available to play, 3 letter words for kid unless you are beginner and higher means harder.

As soon you type a word for that supported length it show how many of the characters of the typed word matched with the hidden word. Green means that letter is matched with the hidden word in the same position, orange means that letter present in the word but not in the correct position, darker colour means that letter does not present in the hidden UK word.

So type your next word wisely, you have total 6 tries to find that hidden British word.

3UK English Spelling Families

Word list

See the main six differences between the spellings of English in UK English and the US English, if you learn all the six differences, you will not face any confusion playing Wordle UK.

  • OUR not OR — COLOUR, FAVOUR, HONOUR, LABOUR, RUMOUR, VAPOUR, VIGOUR, ARMOUR at 6 letters, ODOUR at 5, HARBOUR and PARLOUR at 7
  • RE not ER — LITRE, METRE, MITRE, TITRE, SABRE, OCHRE, FIBRE, NITRE at 5 letters, CENTRE, SOMBRE, LUSTRE at 6, THEATRE and CALIBRE at 7
  • CE not SE — DEFENCE, OFFENCE, LICENCE at 7 letters, PRETENCE at 8. These are nouns, the verb forms still take an S
  • ISE and YSE not IZE and YZE — REALISE, ADVISE, ANALYSE, PARALYSE. Because of this, Z is a much rarer letter in UK mode than in the American version
  • Double L, except when single — TRAVELLED and JEWELLERY gain an L, but ENROL, FULFIL, INSTIL, APPAL and SKILFUL lose one. It runs the opposite way to what most players expect
  • One-off British spellings — TYRE, KERB, GAOL, GREY, MOULD, MATHS, STOREY, WHISKY, PLOUGH, CHEQUE, DRAUGHT, PYJAMAS, SULPHUR, PROGRAMME, ALUMINIUM

To win the game you have to understand the patterns that you get almost nowhere. UK English has a whole family of irregular past tenses ending in T where US English uses ED, when you type Words like BURNT, SPELT, SPILT, LEAPT, SMELT, KNELT and DWELT are all five letters, LEARNT, DREAMT and SPOILT are six. If you get a green T at the end of the word, type the words that have T in the end take it as a tip.

4UK Words

Vocabulary

UK English spelling is only part of it, actually there is a huge layer of everyday UK English vocabulary where an American word list does not have it, and also another layer of regional words that even national dictionaries treat as an afterthought. Both Words are in our Word list.

  • 3 letters — LOO, TIN, BIN, MUM, NAN, BAP, COB, HOB, PEG, MAC, TEA
  • 4 letters — MATE, QUID, POSH, DAFT, NAFF, PINT, BREW, PRAM, CHAP, NOWT
  • 5 letters — LORRY, QUEUE, CRISP, TELLY, BLOKE, DODGY, NAPPY, WELLY, SCONE, PASTY, BARMY, LURGY, PLONK, MARDY, BAIRN
  • 6 and 7 letters — BROLLY, BANTER, GUTTED, PETROL, JUMPER, GEEZER, CHUFFED, TROLLEY, BISCUIT, CRUMPET
  • Regional — BAIRN, BRAW, CANNY, LOCH, DRAM and BONNY from Scotland, NOWT, OWT, MARDY, GINNEL and BARM from the North, CWTCH, TIDY and LUSH from Wales

A dictionary that where you will not find BAIRN is as a British Word. If a word you use every day is missing from the Word List, write it in the comments and we will look for adding it.

5Best UK Wordle Starting Words

Strategy

The Wordle tip you search on the Internet is the Tips for Wordle US English, where it is said to open with AISLE. It is not wrong after all but it is just not actually the best tip for a British word list. See, these pairs cover the vowels first, then the consonants that actually appear most in British English, and are picked for the exact word length you are playing.

  • 3 letters — first word SAT, second PIN. Two vowels and six common consonants in six letters
  • 4 letters — open word RATE, then SILO. Covers A, E, I, O plus R, T, S, L
  • 5 letters — First word SLATE, then second CHOIR. All five vowels across two guesses, plus S, L, T, C, H, R
  • 6 and 7 letters — Start with MASTER then put second word CLOUDY at six, or PAINTER at seven
  • UK only trick — Word with a green R and E in the last two slots means a family of American word lists do not have: LITRE, METRE, MITRE, TITRE, SABRE, OCHRE, FIBRE, NITRE

One final tip to remember or look at while playing In six letter mode, if you typed a word OUR at the end, the chance of being the hidden word can be from words: COLOUR, FAVOUR, HONOUR, LABOUR, RUMOUR, VAPOUR, VIGOUR, ARMOUR and SAVOUR.

The Daily word rolls over at midnight in your own local time, so if you are in the UK that is midnight GMT in winter and midnight BST in summer — you do not have to work out any time difference. Does Wordle UK accept COLOUR and COLOR?

Both words are recognised as valid guesses, but in Wordle UK the words list comes from only the British list we have created.

Is Wordle UK free to play?

Yes. You do not need an account and also unlimited.

Why was my word not accepted?

If your word is not accepted as a valid word then the main reason is the word is not in our Wordle UK word list. You can add the word by putting it in the words suggestion comment below.

Can I play three and four letter Wordle UK?

Yes, you can play Wordle UK three letter, 4 letter and up to 7 letter words, choose the word length from the settings.

Is Wordle UK safe for children?

Yes. We have Kid mode that is built only for the kids after filtering the foul words and it is available in 3 letter words.

Can I switch back to the standard word list?

You can, in Settings choose the region, the game starts immediately with the region word lists.

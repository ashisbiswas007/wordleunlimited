1Gameplay

How to play

Wordle Unlimited is easy to play word game even a kid can play, to play first understand the rule. Our game has 3 main modes and in every mode you have to guess the hidden word in 6 try, see the word length that can be from 3-7 letters for easy to hard. Write the first word and see if any letter changed the color or not, green means the hidden word also have the same letter in the same letter position, orange means the letter present in the word but it is in the wrong place right now and black or dark color means the letter does not contains in the hidden word. Now you have proper hints now write your next word smartly and try to find the hidden word in total 6 tries.

How the colors work

WORDS

Every letter green — correct spot, you win

PLANT

Orange L — right letter, wrong spot

CHAIR

All gray — none of these letters are in the word

You can also enable hints from the settings, click the top right corner gear icon and enable hints. Once enabled, you can find it on the top as bulb icon, tap it to see the hint or tip. Total two hints or tips are available to make the game more interesting.

8Game Modes

The default is Wordle Unlimited, and alongside it you get Daily, Time, Topics and Versus. Lets take a closer look at them to understand better which is going to easy for you to decide what mode to play.

01 · One a day

Daily

Wordle Daily mode where in every 24 hours players get a secret word to solve. Available in all word lengths from 3 to 7 characters, choose in settings. Win or lose records are saved in Stats, and a countdown is shown for the next Daily word.

02 · Most popular

Unlimited

This mode can be played unlimited times as many times you want. If you have any difficulty solving it just tap the reload button to restart with a new word. You also get unlimited hints, and this is the mode with “Challenge a friend”.

03 · For experts

Time

This mode lets you solve within a fixed time. You choose the time, you choose the word length, and make the best record for how many words you can find. Options are 1 minute, 1 and a half, and 2 minutes.

04 · Themed

Topics

Hundreds of themed packs. Guess your way through the names in a topic, one after another, with the length changing as you go.

05 · Live

Versus

Real players, same words, live leaderboard. Join an open room instantly or make a private one for friends.

9Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about Wordle Unlimited.

Is Wordle Unlimited free to play?

Yes. It is completely free, there is nothing to download and no account to create.

How many times can I play in a day?

Unlimited. Only the Daily puzzle is once per day — every other mode you can play as much as you like.

Can my child play Wordle Unlimited safely?

Yes. Switch Word Length to the kid icon in Settings for 3-letter words from a filtered, age-appropriate list. There is no chat, no sign up and no ads aimed at children.

Do I need an account to play multiplayer?

No. Pick a nickname and you are in. Sign in with Google only if you want your stats and level to follow you between devices.

Can I play in British English or Indonesian?

Yes. Use the Language / Region control in Settings, or go straight to Wordle UK or Wordle Indonesia.

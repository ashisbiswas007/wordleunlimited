Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 July 2026.

Wordle Unlimited is a free word game. You do not need an account to play it, and we do not sell or share personal data. This page explains exactly what is stored and where.

The short version

  • Your stats, streaks, level and settings are saved in your own browser, not on our servers.
  • Signing in with Google is optional. If you do, your save file goes into a private folder in your Google Drive that only this app can see. We never see your other files.
  • Multiplayer nicknames are temporary and disappear when the room ends.
  • Challenge links contain the word inside the link itself — nothing is stored on a server.

What is stored in your browser

The game uses your browser's local storage to remember things between visits. This never leaves your device unless you turn on cloud save. It includes your settings (theme, word length, hard mode, hints, sound), your statistics and guess distribution per mode and word length, your level and win count, your best Time-mode scores, any challenge you have been sent but not yet played, and your multiplayer nickname and avatar choice.

Clearing your browser data clears all of it. There is no undo, so use cloud save if you want a backup.

Cloud save with Google Drive

If you choose to sign in with Google, we request two things: your basic profile so we can show you are signed in, and the drive.appdata permission. That permission gives access to a hidden, app-specific folder in your Drive — it does not give access to your documents, photos or any other file, and it is not technically possible for us to read them with it.

A single file called wordle-save.json is written there containing the same data listed above. You can revoke access at any time from your Google account permissions, or by signing out in Settings.

Multiplayer

When you join a live room we hold your nickname, chosen avatar, score and progress in memory for as long as the room exists. When the round finishes we keep a record of the match result — nicknames, scores and the topic played — so we can show recent winners and so the admin can see how the game is being used. No IP address is attached to that record.

Please do not put personal information in your nickname; it is visible to everyone in the room.

Server logs and abuse prevention

Like any website, our server briefly processes your IP address to deliver pages and to apply rate limits that stop abuse. We do not build profiles from it and we do not use it for advertising.

Cookies

The game itself sets no tracking cookies. One cookie is used on the /admin area to keep the site owner signed in. That is a strictly necessary cookie and is never set for ordinary visitors.

Children

The game is suitable for children and includes a kid mode with a filtered word list. There is no chat anywhere in the game, no sign-up requirement, and no advertising aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The only third party involved is Google, and only if you choose to sign in for cloud save. In that case Google's own privacy policy applies to their handling of your account. Share buttons open WhatsApp, Facebook or Telegram in a new tab only when you tap them; nothing is sent to them otherwise.

Your choices

  • Play without signing in — this is the default and nothing leaves your device.
  • Sign out of cloud save at any time in Settings.
  • Clear your browser storage to erase everything the game remembers.
  • Revoke Drive access from your Google account permissions page.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on our site.
